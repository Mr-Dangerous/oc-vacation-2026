🏖️ 28th Street Beach — Ocean City, MD

Surf cam · outdoor shower · sandbar. Best visited around low tide. ✓ Scheduled Aug 12 ✓ Scheduled Aug 14

🌊 How to Use the Tide Info

  • Arrive ~1 hour before low tide for the most exposed sandbar
  • The sandbar is best from 1 hr before → 2 hrs after low tide
  • Tides shift ~50 min later each day — midweek aligns perfectly with late afternoon
  • Check the 28th St surf cam before you go: Surfline OC
  • NWS forecast: weather.gov Ocean City MD
